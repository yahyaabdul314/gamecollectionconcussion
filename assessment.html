Assessment Protocols

🎯 Baseline Assessment

When to perform: Before sports season or when healthy (no recent injuries)

Purpose: Establish individual baseline scores for comparison after potential injury

Recommended: Complete all 5 modules for comprehensive baseline

⚕️ Post-Injury Assessment

When to perform: Within 24-72 hours of suspected concussion, only if cleared by medical professional

Purpose: Document symptoms and functional deficits

Note: Should not be performed if experiencing severe symptoms

📈 Recovery Monitoring

When to perform: During recovery period (as directed by healthcare provider)

Purpose: Track improvement and readiness to progress in return-to-activity protocol

Frequency: Every 3-7 days or as recommended by medical professional

✅ Return-to-Play Clearance

When to perform: Before final medical clearance

Purpose: Verify return to baseline functioning

Requirement: Scores should return to baseline levels or better

💡 Best Practices

  • Complete assessments in a quiet, distraction-free environment
  • Ensure VR headset is properly calibrated and fitted
  • Take breaks between modules if needed
  • Be honest about symptoms - there are no "wrong" answers
  • Establish baseline when healthy for accurate comparison
  • Results are automatically saved to your browser's local storage
  • Consider having a trained observer present during balance tests
